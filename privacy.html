Information we collect

When you request a quote on this website, we collect the information you enter: your name, address, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, the type of insurance you're interested in, and any notes you include.

How we use it

We use this information only to contact you about your insurance needs and to prepare a quote. If you agree on the form, we may contact you by phone call, text message, or email. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time; for texts, reply STOP.

How it's handled

  • Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree, a form-processing service.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We may share information with Farmers Insurance as needed to provide a quote or policy you request.
