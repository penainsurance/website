Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Information we collect

When you request a quote on this website, we collect the information you enter: your name, address, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, the type of insurance you're interested in, any notes you include, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, prepare a quote, and service your insurance. We contact you by phone call or email, and by text message only if you have opted in.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number to send messages related to your quote, appointments, and policy service. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Text STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms of Service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as our messaging platform) that deliver messages on our behalf.

How information is handled

  • Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree, a form-processing service.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We may share the information needed to provide a quote or policy you request with Farmers Insurance. This never includes your text-messaging opt-in data or consent.

Your choices
